## 1. The professional toolkit: solar and geodetic, NOT magnetic

**Indonesia (Kemenag).** Qibla measurement is a formal government service. A mosque applies, a Tim Hisab
Rukyat measures, and the ministry issues a **"Sertifikat Penentuan Arah Kiblat"** within 1 to 3 working
days. The ministry's own Hisab Rukyat coordinator states **the theodolite is the most accurate method**
(Walisongo University thesis). In 2026 Kemenag ran a national campaign with **725,669 registered
locations** for free qibla verification. ✅

**Malaysia (JAKIM).** The 2017 media statement lists the recognised methods: theodolite, compass, the
sun-over-Kaaba method (istiwa' a'dham), star observation, and sunset. Malaysia's reference azimuth band is
**291° to 293°** from true north. ✅

**Turkey (Diyanet).** Runs a formal "Kıble Tespiti" service through the national e-Government portal.
Turkish geomatics engineers have computed the qibla for **every large-scale map sheet in Türkiye using
Vincenty on the ellipsoid**, so urban planners can put it in map legends; their motivation is that mosques
mis-set at planning stage cause costly revisions. ✅

**The gold standard, astrogeodetic survey.** NTUA Athens surveyors measuring 11 historical Greek mosques:
GPS for coordinates, **Polaris observations for true north**, robotic total stations at 200 to 400 points
per building, least-squares fit of the prayer-hall axis. Azimuths recovered to **1 to 7 arc-minutes**. ✅

## 2. What professionals think of magnetic compasses

**This is the heart of the report.**

- **JAKIM, official, 2017:** simple tests of phone qibla apps found errors **"between 3° and 45° depending
  on the app and the phone"**, affected by electromagnetic attraction from iron and electricity. App
  results "only count as an estimate (anggaran)"; for accurate determination, refer to state Mufti offices.
  ✅
- **A US licensed land surveyor**, orienting a mosque in Tennessee: the client's pocket qibla compass
  alarmed him ("Panic almost set in... to orient a mosque...?"). He instead computed the true-north bearing,
  applied NOAA declination (4°04′W), set the line with a Gurley transit, and checked by plunging the scope.
  He notes several sources state **"it is not advisable to determine qibla using a compass"**. ✅
- **Bandung field study:** "most mosque buildings merely make use of a magnetic compass to locate qibla
  direction without firstly carrying out calibration"; largest deviation found 7°06′. ✅
- **Turkey, the drift proof:** Barmore (1985, *JNES*) showed Ottoman mosque orientations can be used as a
  **record of historic magnetic declination**, because mosques set by compass encode the declination of
  their construction era, which is now wrong. ✅

## 4. The Indonesian 2010 fatwa episode, reconstructed

1. **March 2010:** MUI Fatwa **3/2010** said that because Indonesia lies east of the Kaaba, the qibla is
   **"menghadap ke arah barat"**, face west.
2. **The problem:** Indonesia is southeast of Mecca, so the true great-circle qibla is **west-northwest,
   about 292° to 295°**. Facing due west from Indonesia points at Somalia, over 1,000 miles off.
3. **July 2010:** after study with astronomy experts, MUI issued **Fatwa 5/2010** revising the wording to
   **"menghadap ke barat laut"**, face northwest, varying by locality.
4. **Crucially, the qibla itself never changed.** T. Djamaluddin (astrophysics research professor, LAPAN,
   member of Kemenag's Badan Hisab Rukyat) published the definitive clarification: *"There is no mechanism
   on Earth that causes a change or shift in the qibla direction. Earthquakes related to plate movement do
   not change the qibla. The many mosques whose direction is not toward the Sacred Mosque are caused not by
   a changed qibla but by inaccurate measurement at the time they were built."* ✅
5. **Validity ruling:** prayers already said remain valid; mosques need not be demolished. Fatwa 5/2010
   prescribes **realigning the prayer rows (shaf) without demolishing the building**. MUI's Ma'ruf Amin:
   "God understands that humans make mistakes."

## 5. The actual tolerance numbers

**There is no single universal number.** Authorities split into a strict camp and a lenient classical camp.

| Authority | Tolerance | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Egypt, Dar al-Ifta'** | **±45°** | Fatwa 14611: the four directions each span 90°, so the permitted deviation is a quarter-circle; a mosque 13° off need not change ✅ |
| **Malaysia (National Fatwa Committee)** | **3°** | for mosques and surau; Shafi'i ayn al-qiblah influence ✅ |
| **Pahang 2026** | new: 291-293°; **old mosques tolerable to 23°** | past prayers never repeated, "ijtihad is not overturned by a new ijtihad" ✅ |
| **Perlis 2018** | existing buildings may remain **if ≤45° off** | ✅ |
| **Indonesia (MUI)** | no numeric angle | realign rows, not demolition ✅ |
| **Indonesian practitioner norm** | **~2°** | Djamaluddin: under 2 to 4° is not visible in prayer rows 🟡 |
| **Mathematical** | **0°04′ to 0°24′** | the angular half-width of the Kaaba, and of Mecca, as seen from the worshipper ✅ |

**Ibn Taymiyya's classical argument**, quoted in both MUI Fatwa 5/2010 and Dar al-Ifta 14611: if a prayer
row a mile long in Syria had to point at the Kaaba's exact body, each worshipper would need to bend by "the
width of a barley grain". Islam excuses such minutiae, so for the distant, **facing the direction
suffices**.

Modern quantitative framing: at Malaysia's ~5,000 km, each 1° of error displaces the line ~180 km at the
Kaaba.

## 6. Magnetic declination in practice

**The IKN study** (Alawiya & Fadhel 2024): reconstructed declination at Indonesia's new capital from
**1590 to 2024** using NOAA's calculator. **77.18% of the historical values exceed the qibla tolerance of
0.4°**, so for most of history an uncorrected compass there would produce a religiously significant error.
✅

**How practitioners correct it:** add or subtract local declination to convert a true-north azimuth into a
compass reading. The Turkish site namazvakti.com publishes all three values explicitly per city:
"Coğrafi Kuzeyden Kıble Açısı 152° / Magnetik Sapma 6° / **Pusula Kuzeyinden Kıble Açısı 146°**".
**This is the model an honest phone app should follow.** ✅

Classical pocket qibla-compass booklets carried per-city index numbers **revised every ~10 years** as
declination drifted.

## 7. Historical accuracy

**David A. King** (Goethe University Frankfurt) is the reference historian.

- **Al-Khalili's universal qibla table** (Damascus, c. 1365): the qibla for every degree of latitude 10 to
  56°N and longitude 1 to 60° from Mecca, **over 4,000 entries, mostly exact or in error by only 1 to 2
  arc-minutes.** The strongest proof of how precise pre-modern calculation was: limited by coordinates, not
  by mathematics. ✅
- **Al-Biruni (1025 CE):** "if the distance from the Ka'ba is small, its direction may be determined by a
  diligent seeker, but when the distance is great, only the astronomers can determine that direction."
- Early mosques (622 to 900 CE) came within ±2° in over half of cases and ±5° in over 80%, improving over
  time ⚠️ (the underlying dataset is contested; King rejects it outright).

## 8. Phone app accuracy, measured

- **Malaysian istiwa'-adzam ground-truth test**, 15-16 July 2020: several apps deviated **up to 15°**; the
  most reliable, **Easy Qiblat, is sun-based rather than compass-based, and showed zero deviation across
  all phones.** ✅
- **JAKIM's own test:** app errors **3° to 45°**. ✅
- Theodolite ground truth 292°57′44″ against the 20 most-downloaded apps: most agreed at ~292°. ✅
- iOS qibla apps measured ~8% more accurate than Android. 🟡

## 9. The agent's honest-UI recommendations

1. **Show the number, not just a needle.** Display the true-north azimuth, the local declination, and the
   derived compass azimuth, as namazvakti.com and the Tennessee surveyor both do. "A number is checkable; a
   floating arrow is not."
2. **Offer a compass-free mode.** The zero-deviation app in the Malaysian test was the sun-based one.
3. **Teach the sun check.** Four dates a year the sun verifies the qibla to a fraction of a degree
   anywhere. Kemenag's national campaign is built on these dates.
4. **Be explicit that phones estimate and certified survey establishes**, which is JAKIM's own position.
5. **Never invalidate, always correct gently.** Every authority separates measurement from validity. An app
   that tells users their prayers were invalid contradicts all documented rulings.
6. **Don't over-claim.** "100% accurate Qibla!" is contradicted by every published measurement.
