# O1l-B2 — UK second and third sources (Scotland first, then London boroughs, then England's one-month towns)

Captured 2026-11-11, GMT. Public pages only, no login, no forms, no contact. Engine for
classification: adhan 4.4.6 at `Rounding.None`, TZ Europe/London, dates from the capture strings.
Classifying method: implied twilight depression at the printed minute (the FORM-MAP method), Nov-Feb
core, because a UK isha side cannot separate a table from an angle in one month and full-year
zero-offset preset scoring fails by construction once a summer cap enters (Luton scores 0/365 on
ISNA at zero offset and is a clean 12.2 degree print).

Class is stated as the **effective pair**: the depression a printed minute implies, core spread, and
the July value that shows the summer regime.

## Defects found (all recorded, none corrected silently)

1. **Mawaqit year calendars carry a phantom 2026-02-29.** Every calendar holds 366 days in a 365-day
   year; the extra row is discarded on parse. A fit that trusts the row count is one day long.
2. **Mawaqit `timezone` is unreliable.** Edinburgh Polwarth, Roxburgh Street, Darul Quran and Talimul
   Quran declare `UTC` while printing correct Europe/London wall clock, including BST. Read the clock
   from the values, never the label.
3. **The Mawaqit today block is not the calendar.** The live `times` block carries mosque-level
   adjustments the calendar does not (Luton: fajr -9, sunrise -10, asr +10, maghrib +10, isha +8).
   The calendar is the surface; the block is the mosque's display margin set. This is why a naive
   "gate against the live page" fails on a platform table that is itself sound.
4. **Watford jsmcc prints a fixed UTC+1 clock.** July is right, November is 55 minutes early: the
   calendar never leaves BST. Corrected with +60 on non-BST dates; the correction is provisional
   because the isha spread stays at 2.86 after it.
5. **Stockport Muslim Foundation's DPT install is misconfigured.** Dhuhr is right (11:44 against a
   11:45 solar noon) while sunrise 05:40 and maghrib 17:48 give a 12h08 day at 53.4 N in November.
   The solar columns belong to another latitude. Rejected, not fitted.
6. **Mawaqit slug fallback resolves the wrong mosque.** `mawaqit.net/en/<slug>` returned Madina
   Islamic Center (New York), the Islamic Center of Northern Virginia, Umm Ul Qura Alexandria and a
   Dumfries, Virginia "Badr Community Center" for Scottish queries. Every capture now carries a
   coordinate check; five such data files were deleted and their raw captures kept, prefixed
   `REJECTED-`.
7. **DPT installs that serve all zeros** recur: Dundee Islamic Society again, unchanged from O1k.

## What this proves against the brief

Scotland held four; fourteen more dated surfaces exist, eleven of them year-scale, and the cities
Glasgow, Edinburgh, Paisley, Falkirk, Perth, Stirling and Kilmarnock each carry a second or third
source. The owner's estimate of "50 sources, 49 undiscovered" is not contradicted by anything found
here: every city searched produced at least one, and the towns not yet swept (Motherwell, East
Kilbride, Cumbernauld, Hamilton, Airdrie, Dumbarton) returned nothing under these terms only because
their Mawaqit slugs do not exist, not because their mosques do not print.

Nothing here is complete. Every negative above is "not found under these terms".
