# O1k fit-phase amendment (owner order, 2026-11-10 ~23:00)

Binding on every fit agent from now on; the two in-flight fits already comply.

1. FIT ALL YEARS HELD. Every fitting job uses the FULL span of dated data in hand, not one year.
   One recipe across the whole span, then the per-year breakdown underneath: exact/within-1/worst per
   year, and the between-year stability verdict (parameters frozen vs shifted). Confidence comes from
   the span.
2. HARVEST THE FUTURE where a surface serves it, then fit across it. Known far-horizon surfaces:
   - Kazakhstan muftyat.kz: serves any year (probes render to 2100) - harvest 2030/2040/2050/2075/2100
     for Astana plus one 48N-line city, fit the whole span.
   - Muhammadiyah KHGT API (Indonesia): 2025-2035 any coordinate - harvest the decade at 3 cities,
     fit the span (Kemenag line stays 2026-only; the KHGT line is the multi-year one).
   - Saudi umqserv: Hijri years to 1500H (~Nov 2076) - harvest 1450..1500 in decade steps for Makkah,
     fit the span, verify the 90/120 interval holds to the ceiling.
   - Kyrgyzstan muftiyat.kg JSON API: any window any year - harvest 2027..2035 for Bishkek, fit span.
   - Tajikistan /tj/namaz/ntime: archive 2014-12 to present (143 months) - fit the whole archive as
     one span; the 2019-07-01 dhuhr convention change is the known break to date and split on.
   - Tunisia meteo.tn dated archive: 2020..2026 - harvest the back years for Tunis, fit the span.
   - Oman calendar_page2.asp: 2021-2031 - harvest the remaining unsampled years, fit the decade.
   - Niger BUTIN portal: currentYear-1..+6 per region - harvest the full window for one region, fit.
3. Latitude framing the owner set: near-equator surfaces change little year to year; high-latitude
   surfaces swing most. Report each fit's seasonal amplitude (Jan vs Jul fajr span) beside its
   between-year stability, so confidence reads in both dimensions.
4. Deliverable per deep-span fit: the one recipe, per-year scores, the stability verdict, the seasonal
   amplitude, and the span covered in days.
