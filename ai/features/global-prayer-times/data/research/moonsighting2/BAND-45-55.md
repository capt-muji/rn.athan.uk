# BAND-45-55: naked MoonsightingCommittee against every banked high-latitude surface

T-25 part 2, question 3. Every number below is emitted by `run-all.mjs` into
`data/band-scores.json` (87 surfaces, 26,714 dated rows) using the adhan 4.4.6
`MoonsightingCommittee` preset verbatim - 18/18 angles, the committee's
latitude-and-season curve as the safe bound, the 1/7-night rule at or above 55 N -
at each surface's own coordinates and zone, nearest-minute rounding, no tuning of
any kind. A surface either sits on the curve or it does not.

Two known-answer gates ran before anything else and both reproduced the banked
record digit for digit:

- **Jamiat Ulama Oslo** (the fit8 third line): fajr exact 253/365, within-1
  365/365, isha deviation -4..-1 min (fit8's "MC evening +3.5 floor").
- **London's real published minutes 2027-2038** (4,383 days): fajr mean 2.95,
  worst 8, 43.4 pc within 2; isha mean 4.37, worst 11, 24.1 pc - the o1h study's
  published control, to the hundredth and the tenth of a percent.

**Tromsoe split.** On the 130 honest days (fit8's published ranges applied
verbatim) MC still misses IRN by fajr 60.49 / isha 70.05 mean - but on 116 of
the 365 days the naked preset prints NOTHING: at 69.6 N the polar night kills
the sunrise the 1/7 rule and the curve both hang from, and the library does not
implement the committee's own documented slide-down (decrease latitude by 0.1
degrees until the sun sets). IRN's answer on those days is a synthetic table.
Salekhard shows the same hole: 30 undefined days, and the CDUM table's own
substitution (fajr 07:06 flat, isha ~17:56 flat through the polar night) sits
110+ minutes from where the curve would be. **Any consumer shipping the MC
preset above the polar circle ships a prayer that does not exist for weeks.**

**Iceland.** The curve never wins here either: Felag's 14.2/14.0 angle-derived
night portions sit 36-41 (fajr) and 43-47 (isha) minutes from MC across three
banked years, stable to 0.5 min between years. Iceland is the clearest case
where a real high-latitude publisher solved the band with shallower angles, not
with a curve.

## (d) The London control

London's published minutes are the one truth series: MC fajr 2.95 / isha 4.37
over 4,383 days, January fajr 1.02 / July 0.92, January isha 7.01 / July 2.47.
The January isha gap (7 minutes) is the largest single-season miss in the
control - the curve's isha side sits late against the printed January table all
decade. The ELM flagship (LUPT-exact) scores the same shape (f 2.84, i 4.62,
Jan isha 7.48), confirming o1h's finding that the LUPT construction and the
committee's curve are one family: same Blackburn observations, same fitted
shape, minute-level editorial differences.

## (e) Verdict

1. **In the 45-55 band the curve's fajr is the English construction.** Wherever
   an English surface anchors fajr to sunrise by a table (the LUPT family, 11 of
   18 towns in the O1k form map), naked MC lands within 0.1-3 minutes for the
   whole year - and two town mosques, Reading AMIC and Rochdale Golden, sit on
   the curve to worst 1 minute on both prayers, all 365 days. The sub-45
   seasonal blowup o1h measured against fixed-angle authorities does not appear
   against these tables, because the tables are the same family as the curve.
2. **In the same band the curve's isha is nobody's.** The English interval-isha
   towns (80-90 minutes after maghrib) sit 5-13 minutes under MC's evening
   curve; the continental authorities (Diyanet 18/16, VIN caps, EMB windows,
   IZW, Shoura, Kemenag-family KZ) sit 15-40 minutes off on both prayers with
   January the worst month nearly everywhere. No continental authority in the
   band follows the committee.
3. **Above 55 the committee's own published follower is the only one.** The
   Jamiat Ulama network (7 Norwegian cities, 58-60.4 N) rides the curve at fajr
   0.3 and isha +3.5 floor everywhere; every other Nordic and Baltic surface -
   IRN 16/15, mawaqeet 18/15, the Swedish IF Diyanet engine, Estonia's clamp,
   Moscow and St Petersburg - sits 30-95 minutes off. Aberdeen's July isha 0.03
   is a rule coincidence (Wifaqul's 1/7-night isha cap equals MC's 55 N isha
   rule), not a follower: its fajr is 80 minutes off.
4. **The curve does not exist at the polar edge.** Tromsoe and Salekhard expose
   the preset's silent polar-night hole (116 and 30 undefined days), where the
   committee's documented iterative slide-down is the only rule and no library
   implements it. Iceland, which never goes polar-dark, still rejects the curve
   by 36-47 minutes in favour of its own 14.2/14.0.

A 45-55 deployment of naked MC therefore tracks the English tables' fajr to a
couple of minutes, misses every continental authority's fajr and isha by 15-40,
and must not be offered above the polar circle without a written
undefined-day policy.
