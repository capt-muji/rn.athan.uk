## What the seven closures buy

- **F-KDH07 becomes a rule, not an exception.** The Malaysian federation is 18/18 plus a per-zone
  elevation dip; the fit law 0.0293*sqrt(metres) reproduces the dip on five of six zones within
  0.25 deg and exactly at Kinabalu. KDH07 was never an angle anomaly: its zone is a mountain summit.
- **One Pakistani chart is off its city, and only one.** 24 of 25 land on their own meridian; the
  Hyderabad chart is 1.11 deg east. That is a publisher typo class, not a national convention, and it
  means the HYDERABAD row must be refitted at the corrected meridian before it is quoted.
- **A sign in the record needs correcting.** `o1m-5-asia` writes that KHGT subuh sits "8 minutes
  earlier" than SIHAT; the tables and the physics both give KHGT **later** by 8 (18 deg against
  20 deg). The magnitude, the band and the unit count all agree; only the word is wrong. Recorded as a
  correction for the close.
- **The tawkit isha split is a country parameter and the parameter has four values, not two.** France's
  +85 and Sri Lanka's +71 were not in the index.
- **The Chaguanas class is on two tenants, not one**, and the second (Santo Domingo → America/Caracas)
  is the dangerous shape: a wrong zone that is invisible until one of the two moves.
