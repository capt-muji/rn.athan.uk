# Muslim population by country

Muslim population in millions. Primary figure: World Population Review, "Muslim
Population by Country 2026" (WPR), retrieved 2026-11-10. The Wikipedia table in
"Islam by country" carries the same figure for every row checked (Pakistan 233.0,
India 200.0, Iran 85.7, Sudan 38.6, Saudi Arabia 31.5, Malaysia 20.06, Niger
21.1) except Nigeria. Cross-check: Pew Research
Center, *How the Global Religious Landscape Changed From 2010 to 2020*, Appendix B
(2025), 2020 column, Muslim population derived as the appendix's population times
its Muslim percentage.

The two sources are different vintages: Pew counts 2020, WPR estimates 2026. A
gap of up to 10 percent is therefore vintage and rounding, not disagreement. The
note column marks every country where the gap exceeds 10 percent and names the
Pew figure, so the scorer can see the true range rather than a false point.

The list covers all 73 rows of `../countries.json`. Four rows are not countries
(Diyanet European scheme, Fazilet Profile A, Fazilet Profile B, and the Southern
Africa federation, whose nine member rows appear individually), and Jerusalem
appears as Palestine.

## Southern Africa federation

The federation row in `../countries.json` is nine countries, not one. Summed from
the rows above: South Africa 1.10, Tanzania 19.40, Mozambique 5.80, Malawi 4.00,
Zambia 0.17, Zimbabwe 0.10, Botswana 0.009, Eswatini 0.006, Lesotho 0.003, total
30.59 million. Malawi alone carries a 74 percent spread, so the sum inherits it.

## The twenty largest

The twenty largest are Indonesia, Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, Nigeria, Egypt,
Iran, Turkey, Algeria, Morocco, Iraq, Sudan, Saudi Arabia, Afghanistan,
Uzbekistan, Malaysia, Yemen, Syria, Russia and Niger. Fourteen of the twenty have
two sources within 10 percent. Six do not: Nigeria, Egypt, Sudan, Yemen, Syria and
Russia, each flagged above. Every one of the six turns on a percentage-of-
population estimate rather than on a headcount, so no arithmetic reconciles them
and the study should carry the range.

## Coverage

85 country and territory rows. Every country row in `../countries.json` resolves to
one of them: Jerusalem to Palestine, Northern Cyprus to Cyprus, and the four rows
that are schemes to nothing. The Southern Africa federation's nine members stand
as individual rows, and the rest are countries named inside multi-country rows or
carried because they are among the twenty largest.
