# O1h study constraints (owner ruling, 2026-11-10)

1. Global bodies run NAKED: stock, as published and documented by the body itself. No census-fitted angles, margins, rounding, temkin, offset tables or per-country corrections are ever applied to a global method. No per-country configuration of a body, even where the body permits it: the documented default is the method.
2. Constants a body defines as part of itself (Diyanet temkin, Umm al-Qura isha +90/+120, Moonsighting Committee high-latitude seasons, Karachi's Hanafi asr) stay, because they are the method.
3. Census research lives only on the TARGET side: each country's fitted recipe or published parameters are the reference the methods are scored against, never an ingredient of a method.
4. Harness fairness: where a body documents no rounding, the harness rounds every method identically (nearest minute). Where a body documents its own rounding, that rounding is used.
5. Country-side recipes are the census fits exactly as recorded in policy-layer.v2.4.json and the world table; the five non-angle forms are handled by their actual rules or excluded from angle scoring, never approximated into angles.
