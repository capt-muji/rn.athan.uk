Verdict up front: NO dated prayer-time surface exists online for any of the five

## Andorra

- Presence: real (largest Muslim group Moroccan; ~2,000 of 72,000). NO
  purpose-built mosque; the government refuses to allocate land. Imam Mohamed
  Raguig petitioned the bishop in 2003 (wiki-islam-andorra.html). The community
  runs an Islamic Cultural Center (Arabic lessons, ~50 students) with no web
  presence found; no association domain resolves.
- Catalan terms attempted: "horaris de pregària Andorra mesquita comunitat
  islàmica" (engine-walled), "Centre Cultural Islàmic d'Andorra" (no site).
- Verdict: not found under these terms.

## Liechtenstein

- Presence: real, ~5-10 pc bracket in the Islam-in-Europe table. The country has
  EXACTLY ONE mosque: the Green Mosque (Yeşil Camii), run by Liechtenstein Türk
  Birliği, affiliated with the Turkish Diyanet İşleri Başkanlığı; second body:
  Islamic Community in the Principality of Liechtenstein (Islamische Gemeinschaft
  des Fürstentums Liechtenstein), affiliated to the Dachverband islamischer
  Gemeinden der Ostschweiz und des Fürstentums Liechtenstein
  (wiki-islam-liechtenstein.html).
- The Diyanet affiliation makes this the micro-states' one Diyanet-class
  community surface candidate. But neither LTB nor IGFprintsLIE resolvable as a
  domain: ltb.li, liechtenstein-turk-birligi.li, islam-liechtenstein.li,
  islamischeszentrum.li all fail DNS (curl 000). No Gebetszeiten page reachable.
- German terms attempted: "Gebetszeiten Fürstentum Liechtenstein Moschee Muslime"
  (engine-walled), "Gebetszeiten Vaduz Schaan Moschee" (no site).
- Verdict: not found under these terms. A Diyanet-affiliated mosque with no
  published online table — the Diyanet print channel is offline-first.

## Monaco

- Presence: real, tiny. NO mosque; the community has never obtained official
  recognition as a religious association; congregational prayer happens in
  neighbouring France (Beausoleil) or private rooms (wiki-islam-monaco.html).
- French terms attempted: "horaires de prière Monaco mosquée" (engine-walled);
  no association site resolves.
- Verdict: not found under these terms — the community's own prayer surface is
  physically across the border in Beausoleil.

## San Marino

- Presence: real and organised — the census's best micro-state find. Association
  Al Nur ("The Light"), a.k.a. Islamic Centre of San Marino, founded 2012, led
  by president Cristina Mercadini; maintains a prayer room in Gualdicciolo
  (Castello di Acquaviva) that "has functioned as the only mosque in the
  Republic"; organises DAILY prayers, Friday congregational prayer, weekend
  classes (wiki-islam-sanmarino.html, which cites a San Marino RTV report of
  2016-03-01 and a "Comunicato stampa associazione Al Nur — Iniziato il Ramadan
  a San Marino" release — the association announces Ramadan, but no press or
  page surfaces a dated table).
- No domain resolves (alnur.sm, alnursm.org, islamiccentresanmarino.org,
  alnur.org.sm — DNS dead). Masjidbox has no San Marino listing (API routes
  dead; the /mosques/andorra class page 404s).
- Italian terms attempted: "orari di preghiera San Marino moschea Al Nur"
  (engine-walled), "comunicato Al Nur San Marino ramadan orari" (no site).
- Verdict: not found under these terms. The daily-prayer community exists; its
  times live offline.

## Vatican

- No mosque and no resident Muslim community (wiki-islam-vatican.html); the
  Muslim presence is visiting scholars, diplomats, delegations; a prayer room
  existed in the Vatican Apostolic Library (2025) for visitors. No timetable of
  any kind, no body to publish one. Verdict: negative by absence.
