## The oracle against the USNO on civil twilight

No authority publishes an 18-degree Fajr, but the USNO does publish civil
twilight at -6 degrees. Agreeing there proves the twilight SOLVER, which is
the same code path an 18-degree Fajr uses with a different constant.

| quantity | astronomy-engine minus USNO |
|---|---|
| begin civil twilight | 112/2/0 mean 0.00 |
| end civil twilight | 114/0/0 mean 0.00 |

No library disagreed with the USNO by 2 minutes or more on any of the 60
comparisons, for any of sunrise, transit or sunset.
