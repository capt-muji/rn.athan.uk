The deferred fallback question — not a decision

Dubai112

This ranking decides nothing today. Every country in this ledger already has a derived recipe from its own published data, and that is what the app serves. It answers a different, future question: if some place in the middle of the ocean ever needed a computed timetable — no authority, no mosque, no data at all — which single standard method would be the least-wrong global fallback? The measured answer is that Dubai’s angles sit nearest to what the world’s publishers actually print. The choice itself is deferred until a place with nothing actually needs it.
