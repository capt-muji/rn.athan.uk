=== verdict ===
From `measure-jakim-imsak.mjs`: JAKIM IMSAK 19.93 to 20.08, SUBUH 17.55 to 17.89.
Measured here: Kemenag SUBUH 19.40 to 20.06 across the three cities that pass
the anchor gate, and its IMSAK 21.77 to 22.42.

So the SAME CITED NUMBER, 20 degrees, binds to DIFFERENT ROWS in two
neighbouring countries that print the SAME eight-row set with the SAME
10-minute Imsak gap:
  Malaysia  20 degrees is the IMSAK row; SUBUH is 18 plus a 2-minute ihtiyati.
  Indonesia 20 degrees is the SUBUH row; IMSAK is 10 minutes earlier, which
            falls at about 22 degrees and is NOT a published angle at all.

A concept-to-row map that carries only the row NAME is therefore not
sufficient. The map must bind each CONCEPT to its own parameter, because
`Imsak` is an angle in Malaysia and a derived offset in Indonesia.
