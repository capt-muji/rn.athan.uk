== 3e.3 What the shift is worth to a London user, day by day ==
The same calendar slot in the app's own 2026 year against the published 2027 year, Asr only,
with the sun's own annual drift removed by comparing each to its own year's computed Asr.
  Asr margin shift, 2026 to 2027, distribution over 365 slots: +1: 166 days, +2: 195 days, +3: 4 days
  This is the provider's own change, not the app's. It arrives whether v2.0 ships or not.

== 3e.4 Byte cost of the offline London, stated once ==
  366 slots, two uint8 arrays:   732 raw, 352 gzip, 246 brotli
  the same, run-length encoded:  350 raw, 277 gzip, 208 brotli
  Fajr runs: 88, Isha runs: 87
  Fajr range: 90 to 124 minutes
  Isha range: 66 to 100 minutes
  Both fit uint8, so the table needs no encoding cleverness at all.
