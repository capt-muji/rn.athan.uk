## The decomposition that needs no elevation guess

A horizon dip is ANTISYMMETRIC: it moves sunset later by X and sunrise earlier by X.
A safety margin is SYMMETRIC: it moves both later. So from the two residuals:

  dip component    = (maghribResid - syurukResid) / 2
  margin component = (maghribResid + syurukResid) / 2

The dip component is then inverted to the elevation that would produce it, which is a
measurement of what JAKIM used rather than a guess at what the ground is.
