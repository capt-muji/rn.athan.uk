# R14 part 3C: probing `api.waktusolat.app` `GET /zones/{lat}/{lon}` on a grid

Grid step 0.25 degrees, 1605 points over two bounding boxes covering peninsular
Malaysia and Malaysian Borneo, including surrounding sea. Fetched on the date in the report.

| outcome                                               | points | share |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ------ | ----- |
| a zone was returned                                   | 425    | 26.5% |
| `No zone found for the given coordinates.` (HTTP 500) | 1180   | 73.5% |
| transport failure, retried                            | 0      | 0.0%  |

Most of the no-zone points are open sea inside the two bounding boxes, which is correct
behaviour. What it establishes is that the service refuses rather than guesses, so a user
just offshore or in an unmapped district gets nothing.

Distinct zones the service returned anywhere on the grid: **50** of the 60 JAKIM publishes.

A zone never returned by a coordinate lookup cannot be reached by GPS at all, so those users
need a picker no matter what.

## 3D. Could the mapping ship offline as a nearest-point table?

Leave-one-out nearest-neighbour accuracy over the 425 on-land grid points: **64.0%** (272 of 425).

Storing the grid itself as 2 int16 coordinates plus a 1-byte zone index is 2,125 raw bytes for 0.25-degree resolution, which is about 28 km of cell.
