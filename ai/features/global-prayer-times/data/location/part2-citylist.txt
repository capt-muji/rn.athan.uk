# R14 part 2: the cost of a worldwide city list

GeoNames `cities15000`, downloaded 2026-09-30: 34152 places over 15,000 population, CC BY 4.0.

## 2K. Byte cost of the full 34,152-city list, four encodings

| encoding | raw bytes | gzip bytes | brotli bytes | brotli B per city |
| -------- | --------- | ---------- | ------------ | ----------------- |
| json     | 1,145,114 | 418,591    | 348,671      | 10.2              |
| tsv3     | 949,936   | 411,140    | 341,276      | 10.0              |
| tsv2     | 881,640   | 362,813    | 302,545      | 8.9               |
| bin      | 559,822   | 361,875    | 312,897      | 9.2               |

`bin` stores latitude and longitude as int16 at 0.01 degree, which is 1.1 km of latitude
resolution, well inside the tolerance part 1 establishes.

## 2L. How many cities are needed to cover every one of the 34,152 places within R km

Greedy set cover: repeatedly take the city whose R-km disc contains the most uncovered
population, until every place is covered. This is the honest answer to "how many cities
does the picker actually need".
