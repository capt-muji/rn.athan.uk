# R14 part 5: elevation

## 5A. The horizon dip through adhan itself

adhan has no elevation input. The dip is applied by treating sunset as the sun reaching
`-(0.833 + dip)` rather than `-0.833`, solved the same way. `dip = acos(R/(R+h))` with R = 6,371,000 m.

## 5C. The decisive test: does elevation move the app toward or away from JAKIM?

JAKIM assigns separate zones to high ground: `PHG06` Cameron Highlands, `PRK07` Bukit Larut,
`SBH06` Gunung Kinabalu, `KDH07` Puncak Gunung Jerai (R1, cited; zone names read from
e-solat.gov.my 2026-09-30). If JAKIM already accounts for elevation in those zones, then an app
applying its OWN elevation correction on top would double-count. This is measured in
`part5-jakim.mjs` against JAKIM's own published September 2026 times.
