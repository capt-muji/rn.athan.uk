## 3B. The counts that decide the design

| measure                                     | value                             |
| ------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Muslim-majority countries in `zone1970.tab` | 51                                |
| of those, exactly ONE IANA zone             | **47**                            |
| of those, more than one zone                | 4: ID (4), KZ (7), MY (2), PS (2) |

So for a large majority of Muslim-majority countries the zone and the country carry the
SAME information, which is why the zone is a usable country signal despite the ten
ambiguous cases above.
