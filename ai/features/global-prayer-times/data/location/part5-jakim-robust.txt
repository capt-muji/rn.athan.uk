# R14 part 5C robustness: is the dip real or a coordinate artefact?

JAKIM `period=year` fetched from `e-solat.gov.my`. Dip component per month, defined as
`((publishedMaghrib - computedMaghrib) - (publishedSyuruk - computedSunrise)) / 2`, so a
symmetric safety margin and any longitude error cancel out of it exactly.

## 5C-3. Separating the asymmetric ihtiyati from the elevation

R6 measured JAKIM Kuala Lumpur's per-field offsets as Sunrise -1 and Maghrib +2, which is
itself an antisymmetric pair and therefore lands inside the dip component as
`(2 - (-1)) / 2 = 1.50` minutes of apparent dip at zero elevation. The measured WLY01 value
is the baseline. Subtracting it leaves the part attributable to elevation alone.

Measured WLY01 baseline dip component: 1.36 min. R6's offset pair predicts 1.50 min.
