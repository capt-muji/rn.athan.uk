## 3E-3b. The ceiling, with a hand-written alias table for the spelling variants

The failures above are dominated by orthography: `Kulaijaya` against `Kulai`, `Ulu Langat`
against `Hulu Langat`, `Hulu Perak` covering three JAKIM zones at once. This pass adds an
alias table and measures what a real implementation would reach.

| outcome                                         | points | share |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------ | ----- |
| offline answer equals the service               | 410    | 96.5% |
| one ADM2 polygon spans more than one JAKIM zone | 9      | 2.1%  |
| genuine polygon disagreement                    | 6      | 1.4%  |
