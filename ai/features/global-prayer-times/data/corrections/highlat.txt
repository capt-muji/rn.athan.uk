## I3. What `adhan` returns when the angle has no answer

London at 18 degrees, `TwilightAngle`: `adhan` returned NaN on **0** of 365 days (measured).
So the library never signals failure. It silently substitutes a night-portion value.
On 2026-06-21 it returns Fajr 02:31 and Isha 23:34, both of which are substitutions, not 18-degree times (measured).
