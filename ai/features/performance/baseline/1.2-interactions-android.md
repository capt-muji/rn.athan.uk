# Baseline 1.2 — Interaction suite, Android (OnePlus 3T, gfxinfo per suite)

Harness: Maestro CLI flows (paced by assertion polls); gfxinfo reset before each suite.
Janky % = frames over the 16ms vsync budget rendered during the suite.

| Suite | Frames | Janky | p50 | p90 | p95 | Wall |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sheets open+close ×20 (gear + hardware back) | 1524 | **43.1%** | 15ms | 25ms | 29ms | 149s |
| Toggles ×20 ("Show hijri date" flip) | 459 | **24.4%** | 13ms | 21ms | 27ms | 120s |
| Overlay open+close ×20 (prayer row tap + center tap) | 760 | **69.9%** | 19ms | 25ms | 27ms | 179s |
| Pager swipes ×30 (Standard↔Extras, y=21% band) | 962 | **37.6%** | 14ms | 25ms | 27ms | 97s |
| Sound selects ×20 (Athan 1↔2 rows in sound sheet) | 455 | **66.4%** | 19ms | 29ms | 34ms | 146s |

## Findings

1. **Every suite renders >24% janky frames** — interactions are over-budget across the board.
2. **Overlay is the worst offender (69.9%)** — confirms Phase 3 backlog #3 (SVG glow + ticking
   Countdown subtree always mounted).
3. **Sound sheet spam 66.4%** — confirms backlog #6 (rows re-render against player status ticks).
4. **Sheets 43.1%** — sheet dismissal burst (backlog #2) likely contributes.

## Behavior observations (for 1:1 preservation)

- Backdrop tap does NOT dismiss sheets (design: BottomSheetBackdrop pressBehavior default
  'none'); Android hardware back dismisses; sheets close via back only (no X button).
- **Hardware back from the sound sheet closed BOTH sheets** (sound + settings stack) —
  BackHandler double-registration; capture in behavior snapshot.
- Overlay open covers home visually but Android AX keeps home labels "visible" (assertions
  must use overlay-unique anchors or absence-of-duplicates).
- Pager swipe must happen in the y≈21% band (countdown/date area); row area swipes are
  consumed by row pressables (no paging).
- Sound rows are "Athan 1".."Athan 32"; row tap = select, right-edge play icon = preview.
