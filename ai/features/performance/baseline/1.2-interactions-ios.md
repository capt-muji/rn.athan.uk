## Findings

1. **Sheet open ≈ 2.1s and overlay open ≈ 2.1s** — even after poll-bias subtraction
   (~1.5–1.9s real), both FAIL the instant-feel bar by a wide margin. Cross-check with
   xctrace in 1.4 (poll bias vs render truth).
2. **Pager page-change ≈ 1.0s to content label** — sluggish first paint of the target page.
3. iOS warm-launch-to-content (~1.2s, from 1.1) is consistent with slow first-frame paths —
   likely shares root cause with (1)/(2): heavy mount/first-render work.

## Harness quirks (documented for reuse)

- Sheet content AX-invisible on iOS Release — assert chrome, not content.
- `close_sheets()` needs up to 2 swipes for stacked sheets (sound over settings).
- Android-layout coords scale cleanly to iOS (sheet top 29.9% ≈ 30% on both).
- Overlay ON detection: formatted-date occurrences ≥4 (home=2 with always-mounted overlay
  subtree, +2..4 when big overlay renders).
