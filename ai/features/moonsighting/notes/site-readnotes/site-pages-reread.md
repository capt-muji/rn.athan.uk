### 1. Prayer-time calculation
No page in this set gives a prayer-time method. None mentions a Fajr or Isha angle as a rule, Shafaq, 1/7 of the night, Aqrabul-Bilad, Zuhr, Asr or Maghrib calculation, or a timetable for any place. What there is:
- The only substantive passage is the blurb for Shaukat's book "Fajr & Isha" at https://www.moonsighting.com/3books.html: "the difficult nature of the problems was realized more fully when the Muslim communities established themselves in England, North America, and Northern Europe during the last few decades, especially in towns located beyond 50�N latitudes.  At these locations the days and nights were unusually long in certain months and the twilights did not disappear in summer.  The prayer schedules adapted from the temperate climates did not suit their needs." and "For instance, in the last few years the Ulama in England have switched from 102� to 108� and then back to 102�, etc., and yet no satisfactory answer has emerged3." The aim is stated as "principles which will help us prepare the prayer schedules for the maximum number of locations on earth without deviating from the established Islamic norms." The "�" is the replacement character EF BF BD in the mirrored HTML itself. The numbers are quoted exactly as served, and no corrected value is inferred. Book date, publisher and footnote 3 are not on the page, and the order contact after "Request any Book or all 3 Books by:" is empty.
- Historical remark, https://www.moonsighting.com/ever-wonder.html: "(2) 300 years ago, when clocks and watches were invented; use of those devices for prayer times was not allowed by Fatawa (Fatwas)." and "(3) In 1900 AD, in Indian subcontinent, calculated Prayer Times were not acceptable by Fatawa (Fatwas)."
