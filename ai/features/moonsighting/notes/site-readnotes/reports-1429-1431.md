## 2. Findings by topic

### 2.1 Prayer times, Fajr, Isha, Shafaq, twilight, 1/7 night, Aqrabul-Bilad, offsets, timetables
- NONE of the 36 pages contains a prayer timetable, a Fajr or Isha calculation, Shafaq, twilight angles, the 1/7-night rule, Aqrabul-Bilad, or Zuhr/Asr/Maghrib offsets. After the full read, a word search confirmed that "Fajr", "Shafaq", "Unified", "timetable", "Updated", "Aqrab", "Zuhr", "1/7" and "angle" occur nowhere.
